Data Deletion

Decode Money with Tara · last updated 5 November 2026

Our Instagram app keeps only two small records when you comment a keyword: the comment ID and your Instagram-scoped user ID with the keyword and time. They are deleted automatically after 30 days, so you don't need to do anything. See the privacy policy for details.

Requests and questions

We don't keep your username or any other way to look you up, so there is nothing else to delete: every record is gone at most 30 days after your last keyword comment. If you'd like a confirmation or have a question, send a direct message with the word DELETE to @decodemoneywithtara on Instagram and we will reply.

Messages and comments

The direct message we sent you and our public reply to your comment live on Instagram. You can delete your own comment and the conversation in the Instagram app at any time.
