Privacy Policy

Decode Money with Tara · last updated 5 November 2026

Decode Money with Tara is an Instagram, YouTube and Facebook channel of Hinglish money explainers. Tara is an AI character. This page explains what data our free tools handle: the SIP calculator on this website and the automatic replies on Instagram.

The SIP calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are saved only in your own browser (so they are there next time you open the page) and are never sent to us. We use no cookies, no analytics and no ads on this site.

Instagram comment replies

When you comment a keyword (for example "SIP") on one of our Instagram posts or Reels, our own small app, which works only with the Instagram account @decodemoneywithtara, sends you one direct message with the link you asked for and posts a short public reply under your comment. To do this, Instagram sends our app:

  • your comment's ID and text,
  • your Instagram-scoped user ID and username,
  • the ID of the post or Reel you commented on.

We read the comment only to check for the keyword. We store just two small records: the comment ID (so you never get the same message twice) and your Instagram-scoped user ID with the keyword and the time (so we send at most one message per keyword per day). Both are deleted automatically after 30 days. We do not store your username or your comment text, and we do not build profiles.

Who sees it

Nobody else. We never sell, rent or share this data, and never use it for advertising. It is processed on Netlify, which hosts this website, and through Meta's Instagram Platform, under their own terms.

Your choices

  • Don't comment a keyword, and our app does nothing with your comment.
  • Questions about your data, or a deletion confirmation: see data deletion.

Children

Our content is for adults starting their first jobs. We do not knowingly handle data of children under 13.

Contact

Send a direct message to @decodemoneywithtara on Instagram. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

Education only, not investment advice.
